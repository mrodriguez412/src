Linear regression (eigen) : 330 hz , not much influenced by the scene, 1.3% of the CPU
CERES regression : 350 hz, not much influenced by the scene either, 8.2% of the CPU

For a flat surface : 
	Eigen : z = 0.01x + -0.19y + -0.21
	CERES : z = 0.02x + -0.19y + -0.22
	
So pretty similar precision

Without robustify, CERES is identical to eigen in term of response to objects.
With the huber loss enabled, the regression slightly better fits the ground when objects are near.

Possible improvements would be RANSAC to estimate the dominant ground plane
